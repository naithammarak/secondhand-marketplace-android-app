## What changed and why

`OrderSettlementService.settle` is the only RELEASE/REFUND implementation. HTTP receipt/Admin commands, the post-return hook and all financial jobs call it. Its caller owns commit/rollback; it locks Order → Shipments → Escrow → Product, refreshes authorization, verifies successful Payment/Attempt/Receipt, original money/party relationships, inspection/decision/certificate, selected proof and fresh DB wall clock after I/O. A's shared simulation opt-in guard applies to new financial writes. Production/unknown environments deny; historical authorized replay remains readable.

RELEASE copies the saved allocations: held1350 → seller1140 + commission60 + inspection100 + shipping50, refund0. REFUND returns1350 and retains0. A unique immutable settlement across both kinds, command/history, Order/Escrow/Product, Buyer receipt or Admin resolution share one rollback boundary. Product becomes SOLD or CANCELLED and never auto relists. Original successful charge/receipt and pricing snapshots remain intact; owning list/detail payment_status derives REFUNDED from the committed ledger.

Implemented owning active Buyer receipt/report (including Seller-as-Buyer), delivery/history reads and final detail flags/summary. Receipt before deadline produces COMPLETED/RELEASED. A timely report is immutable DELIVERY_DISPUTED/HELD and blocks AUTO, including during Storage outage. Exact-at/after writes fail using DB time after the Order lock, not request-arrival time.

Admin queue lists only case IDs/times. Review requires a reason, persists idempotent audited access and returns same-case server references. Resolution requires prior own case review, reason and authorized report/selected-proof/audit references. RELEASE requires readable confirmed Buyer proof. REFUND can rely on the persisted non-receipt report even when an object is missing. Audit, resolution and financial outcome commit atomically; general unrestricted customer reads were not added.
